About Debug Club

We are Debug Club, a scientific student club dedicated to learning, creating, and having fun together. We organize practical classes, hands-on workshops, and competitions covering a wide range of fields—including programming, robotics, PC hardware components, graphic design, photography, videography, editing, and competitive problem-solving.

Beyond technical skills, we also organize gaming events and social activities where members can play together, connect, and de-stress from academic life. That is just a small glimpse of what we do!

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior coding experience to join?

No! We welcome beginners and experienced developers alike across all our tracks (programming, robotics, design, editing, and gaming).

What will I gain by joining the club?

You will gain hands-on technical skills across coding, hardware, and creative design, build real projects with a passionate team, compete in hackathons and club tournaments, earn XP on our mobile app leaderboard, and make lasting friendships that go beyond university walls.

How does the ranking system work on the mobile app?

You earn points and XP by participating in workshops, competing in challenges, and joining club activities.

Will joining the club help my professional career?

Absolutely. You will build a solid portfolio through hands-on team projects, gain experience using modern industry tools, and develop critical soft skills like leadership, teamwork, and problem-solving that set you apart in tech job interviews.

Do members receive certificates for participation or training?

Yes, active members receive recognized certificates of participation upon completing specialized workshops, bootcamps, and hackathons.

How can I host a workshop or manage an event within the club?

We actively encourage knowledge sharing! Any member with a passion or skill can submit a workshop proposal or event initiative. Our core team provides full logistical, technical, and organizational support to bring your idea to life.
